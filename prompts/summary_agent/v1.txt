## SUMMARY COMPONENT GUIDE

### executive_summary (2–3 paragraphs)
Paragraph 1: Nature of the agreement + parties + purpose.
Paragraph 2: Key financial terms (value, term, payment structure).
Paragraph 3: The single most important commercial or legal point a decision-maker must know.

### deal_structure (1–2 paragraphs)
Explain the commercial relationship. Who provides what? Who pays what? How is performance measured? What happens on termination?

### key_parties (list of KeyParty objects)
For each party: name, role, and their primary obligation in one sentence.

### key_commercial_terms (list of short strings)
Each item ≤ 15 words. Examples:
- "Annual subscription fee: $120,000 payable in advance"
- "Uptime SLA: 99.0% with service credits on breach"
- "Price increase cap: 15% per renewal cycle"
- "Liability cap: 12 months of fees paid"

### critical_dates (list of KeyDate objects)
Include: effective date, expiry/initial term end, renewal dates, notice deadlines, milestone dates, reporting deadlines.

### governing_law
State the governing law jurisdiction and any specific court/arbitral forum.

### dispute_mechanism
Describe the dispute resolution process: negotiation period → arbitration/litigation, seat, rules, any class action waiver.

## EXAMPLE executive_summary (plain mode)

"This is a 5-year Airport Catering and Meal Service Agreement between Metropolitan Airport Authority of Greater Regions ('Airport Authority') and Global Gastronomy Group LLC ('Caterer'), effective January 1, 2027 and running through December 31, 2031. The Caterer provides all in-flight meals (Economy, Business, and First Class) to airlines operating at the airport, at rates ranging from $12.75 to $85.00 per meal depending on class.

The contract has a heavy performance management framework: 15 numbered KPIs govern everything from on-time delivery (target: 98.5%) to food safety incidents (target: zero), with penalties ranging from $500 per incident up to $1,000,000 for a fatal food safety outcome. The Caterer faces significant penalty exposure if KPI performance degrades.

The most critical commercial point: the contract has no aggregate annual penalty cap. In a worst-case scenario involving food safety incidents and sustained KPI underperformance, total annual penalties could exceed the contract's revenue value. This requires immediate review before signing."
